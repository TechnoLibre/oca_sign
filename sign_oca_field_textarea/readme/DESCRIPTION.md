This module adds the _Multi-line Text_ type to the fields of sign_oca documents.

On the signing page, such a field is a text area that fills its box: Enter starts a new
line, the box scrolls when the text is taller than it, and Tab moves to the next field.
When the document is signed, the text is drawn in the PDF from the top of its box, with
its line breaks; lines wrap to the width of the box, and what passes its bottom is cut.
