- Log in to the portal as a user who has sign requests.
- Open _My Account_ and click on _Signature Requests_.
- Use the search field, its drop-down chooses where to search: _All_, _Document_ or
  _Related Record_.
- Use _Group By_ to group the lines by _Document_, _State_, _Sent by_, or _Sent on_ by
  year, month or day.

The sent date is the date the user was added as a signer of the request.
