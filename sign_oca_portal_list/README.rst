This module enriches the portal list of sign requests
(``/my/sign_requests``):

- the sent date and the signature date are shown down to the second
  (``yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss``), in the time zone of the user;
- each line shows the related record of the request, when its name
  differs from the document's, and who sent it;
- on a narrow screen the table keeps the document and its state; a line
  under the document gives the related record, the sent date and the
  sender;
- a search field looks in the document name, in the display name of the
  related record, or in both;
- the lines can be grouped by document, state, sender, or sent date by
  year, month or day; a group never splits across a page;
- the pager keeps the sorting, filter, search and grouping in its links.

The "Signature Requests" tile of ``/my`` is always shown, even when the
user has no request yet.

Usage
=====

- Log in to the portal as a user who has sign requests.
- Open *My Account* and click on *Signature Requests*.
- Use the search field, its drop-down chooses where to search: *All*,
  *Document* or *Related Record*.
- Use *Group By* to group the lines by *Document*, *State*, *Sent by*,
  or *Sent on* by year, month or day.

The sent date is the date the user was added as a signer of the request.
